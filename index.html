A quiet pause in your day

Chai Corner is imagined as a warm stop for students, friends, and families who want proper Pakistani chai, light bites, and a calm place to sit. This website is a student demonstration project — not a live business listing.

  • Hand-brewed chai blends
  • Fresh snacks made daily
  • Welcoming seats for work or conversation
