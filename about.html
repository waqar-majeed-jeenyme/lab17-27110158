Our Story

Built around the daily cup

Chai Corner is presented here as a modern Pakistani café concept for a university lab demonstration. The narrative below is fictional background for design practice, not a verified business history.

How the idea started

The concept imagines a small team that wanted a café where chai is the centrepiece — not an afterthought. Guests could arrive for a quiet morning cup, a quick snack between classes, or an evening catch-up with friends.

What makes it feel like home

Warm lighting, rounded seating nooks, and a menu that stays familiar: doodh patti, samosas, and a few sweets that pair well with chai. The look stays cream, brown, and orange — calm, not loud.

Brand values

Principles that guide the Chai Corner demonstration brand.

Warmth

Every guest should feel welcomed within the first minute.

Care

Simple recipes done carefully beat complicated menus done poorly.

Belonging

A café for conversation, study, and everyday rituals.
